Sunesis

is a noun derived from the Greek word sýnesis (σύνεσις), meaning understanding, intelligence, or sound judgment.
    In more precise terms, sunesis refers to:
  • The ability to comprehend situations clearly.
  • Practical wisdom applied to real-life decisions.
  • Good sense or discernment, especially in complex matters.
